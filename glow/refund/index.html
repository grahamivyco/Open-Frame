All purchases in Glow are processed by Apple or Google. We don't have access to your payment details and can't issue refunds directly.

How to request a refund

iPhone / iPad (App Store):

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID
  3. Pick "Request a refund" and choose the purchase

Android (Google Play):

  1. Go to play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory
  2. Find the purchase and select "Request a refund"

Google may refund automatically within 48 hours of purchase. After that, contact us and we'll help where Google allows.

Missing items

If you paid but didn't get your item, try restoring purchases in the Shop first. If that doesn't fix it, email grahamivy.co@gmail.com with your order ID and we'll make it right.

EU / UK players

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content, but it ends once you start using the content and you agreed to this before purchase.

Refunded items

If a purchase is refunded, we may remove the related items or currency from your account.
