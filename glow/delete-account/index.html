Glow Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceRefund PolicyDelete your data

You can delete your Glow account and all data tied to it at any time. Glow is made by Open Frame.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Glow and go to Settings
  2. Tap Delete account
  3. Confirm

Delete without the app

Email grahamivy.co@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glow data". Include your display name and, if you signed in, the email address you used. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your account, anonymous player ID, display name, email address and sign-in IDs, scores and leaderboard entries, orbs, gems, unlocked items, quests, achievements and streaks.

What we may keep

Purchase records that the law requires us to keep, for up to 7 years. Unused virtual items are lost and can't be refunded.
