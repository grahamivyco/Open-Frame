Open Frame ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Glow (the "Game"). This policy explains what data the Game collects, why, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

When you play without signing in: the Game creates an anonymous ID so your progress saves. We store your scores, orbs, gems, unlocked cosmetics, quests, achievements and daily streak with that ID. We don't know who you are.

Display name: you get a generated name like "GlowRunner". Players 13 and older can change it. It shows on the public leaderboard.

If you choose to sign in (13+ only) with Apple, Google or email, we store your email address and the sign-in provider's account ID so you can restore progress on other devices.

Purchases: Apple or Google send us a receipt. We never see your card details.

Ads: we show banner ads from Google AdMob on menu screens. For players 13 and older, AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address and device info to show and measure ads, after asking consent where the law requires it (and on iOS, tracking permission). For players under 13, ads are non-personalized and child-directed.

Technical data: like most online services, our servers log IP address, device type and app version when the Game connects, to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data or chat messages. We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Game and save your progress
  • To process and restore purchases
  • To fix bugs and improve the Game
  • To show ads (personalized only for players 13+ who agree)
  • To answer support requests

We do not sell your personal data.

Legal bases (EEA and UK players): we use your data to provide the Game you asked for (contract), to show personalized ads only with your consent (consent, which you can withdraw anytime), to keep the Game secure and fix bugs (legitimate interests), and to keep purchase records the law requires (legal obligation).

4. Tracking and ads

We never track children. For players 13 and older, on iOS we ask permission before tracking you across other apps (App Tracking Transparency). In the EU, UK and similar regions we ask consent before personalized ads. You can change this anytime in your device settings.

5. Children

Glow can be played by children, including children under 13. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR rules for children, and the Apple Kids and Google Play Families policies.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • When the Game first opens, we ask for the player's birth year without suggesting an answer. The year stays on the device; we only use it to decide whether the player is under 13. Players under 13 get the kid-safe version below.
  • For children: no personalized or behavioral ads, no cross-app tracking, no chat, no sign-in or email collection, no custom display names, and no links to outside payment pages.
  • Ads shown to children come from Google AdMob with child-directed treatment turned on: non-personalized and rated for general audiences.
  • For children we only keep what's needed to run the Game: the anonymous ID, game progress and the generated display name. None of it is used for advertising.
  • Parents can review or delete their child's data, refuse further collection, or withdraw consent, by emailing grahamivy.co@gmail.com or using Settings > Delete account. We respond within 30 days.
  • If we learn we collected a child's personal information without consent, we delete it.

6. Retention

We keep data while your account is active, or for 24 months after your last play, then delete it. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days. Purchase records may be kept longer where the law requires.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your data.

  • In the Game: Settings > Delete account
  • On the web: openframe.design/glow/delete-account
  • By email: grahamivy.co@gmail.com

We respond within 30 days. Players in the EEA and UK can also ask us to restrict or stop processing, request a portable copy of their data, and complain to their local data protection authority.

California and other US states: in the last 12 months we collected identifiers (anonymous ID, email if you signed in, advertising ID for players 13+), purchase records, game activity, and technical data, for the purposes in section 2. We do not sell personal information. For players 13 and older, AdMob's use of the advertising ID for personalized ads may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising. You can opt out anytime: on iPhone, turn off Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, use Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID. You can also email us to opt out, know, correct or delete your data. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards. No system is perfectly secure.

9. International transfers

Data is stored in the United States, where our service providers operate, with appropriate safeguards.
